Content & Skills

Anyone with an interest in bioinformatics and life sciences: undergraduate, Master's, and PhD students from any life-science background (Biochemistry, Molecular Biology, Biotechnology, Pharmacy, Genetic Engineering, Microbiology, and more), early-career researchers, and complete beginners.

No. A basic biology background and curiosity are enough. The program starts from the fundamentals and builds up to full research workflows step by step.

No. This internship is 100% tool-, database-, and software-based. You will not write a single line of code. Everything is done through ready-to-use web databases and user-friendly software.

You will work with widely used — and mostly free — web databases and software: NCBI, UniProt, PDB, AlphaFold, PubChem, ZINC, Swiss Drug Design, ADMETlab, ProTox, BIOVIA Discovery Studio, AutoDock Vina, SwissDock, STRING, Cytoscape, GeneCards, GEPIA2, UALCAN, GREIN, C-ImmSim, Gaussian, and more.
